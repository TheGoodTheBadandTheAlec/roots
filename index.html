Research & data

How the genealogy is constructed

A link between genres represents a documented or plausible musical ancestry. It does not always establish direct contact between named artists. Early figures can be credited founders, notable pioneers, or later interpreters. Origin periods are approximate.

Listening selections are editorial recommendations based on historical influence and stylistic representation. No objectively provable top-five ranking exists across all musical traditions. Some styles are better represented by singles or performances than albums.

The atlas currently includes profiles with named early innovators, listening suggestions, mapped ancestors and source links when available. The audit identifies research gaps at record level.
